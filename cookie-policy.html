Cookie Policy

1. Introduction

1. Introduction

Botanika, its associates, affiliates, and partners (“we,” “us,” “our”) use cookies and similar technologies to enhance user experience, personalize content, analyze website traffic, and deliver targeted advertising. This Cookie Policy explains what cookies are, how we use them, and your rights and options regarding cookie preferences.

2. What Are Cookies?

2. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device (computer, tablet, smartphone) when you visit websites. Cookies enable websites to remember your preferences, identify your device, and improve your browsing experience.

3. Types of Cookies We Use

3. Types of Cookies We Use

A. Necessary Cookies

Necessary cookies are essential for basic website functionality, including user login, maintaining shopping cart contents, and facilitating secure payment processing. These cookies do not require your consent and cannot be disabled, as doing so would impair website functionality.

B. Functional Cookies

Functional cookies allow Botanika’s website to remember your preferences, such as your chosen language and currency settings. This enhances your browsing experience by personalizing content.

C. Analytics Cookies

We use analytics cookies, specifically Google Analytics, to gather statistical data about how users interact with our website. These cookies help us improve our website performance and usability by providing insights such as user traffic, page visits, and navigation patterns.

D. Marketing Cookies

Marketing cookies are used to deliver targeted advertising relevant to your interests. We employ third-party marketing cookies provided by Google Ads and Facebook Pixel (Meta Pixel) to help tailor ads, track the effectiveness of advertising campaigns, and provide relevant advertisements to users.

4. Cookie Duration

4. Cookie Duration

The cookies used by Botanika are primarily session-based, meaning they expire when you close your browser. Session cookies ensure that your experience is seamless during each browsing session.

5. Third-Party Cookies

5. Third-Party Cookies

We utilize third-party services such as Shopify and Stripe, which may also place cookies on your device to provide their services (payment processing, e-commerce functionality). Additional third-party services may be included in the future; any updates will be reflected in this policy.

6. Cookie Consent and Your Rights

6. Cookie Consent and Your Rights

Upon visiting our website, you will encounter a cookie banner that clearly informs you about our cookie usage and seeks your explicit consent for Functional, Analytics, and Marketing cookies. Necessary cookies are always enabled by default. You have the right to opt-out and withdraw your consent for non-essential cookies (Functional, Analytics, and Marketing) at any time. You can manage your cookie preferences directly through the cookie banner on our website.

7. Managing Cookies

7. Managing Cookies

Most web browsers also allow you to manage cookie settings directly. You can typically find these settings in your browser’s options or preferences menu. Please note that disabling cookies may limit some website functionality and impact your overall user experience.

8. Changes to This Cookie Policy

8. Changes to This Cookie Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy periodically to reflect changes in our practices or regulatory requirements. We encourage you to review this policy regularly for the latest information on our cookie practices.

9. Contact Us

9. Contact Us

For questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Cookie Policy or cookie usage, please contact us at: support@botanika.ai.
